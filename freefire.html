Free Fire Esports

BattleGrid Free Fire is a daily competitive tournament where players can participate in Solo or Duo matches.


Players can choose the match time according to their availability and register directly for that match.


Make sure your Game UID and In-Game Name are entered correctly during registration.

Match Format

  • Game: Free Fire
  • Mode: Solo / Duo
  • Solo Games: 12 Daily
  • Duo Games: 12 Daily
  • Platform: Mobile
  • Registration: Online

Tournament Rules

Read before registering

  • Enter your correct Free Fire Game UID.
  • Enter the correct In-Game Name (IGN).
  • Duo registration requires two players.
  • Players must join their assigned match on time.
  • Players must follow BattleGrid tournament instructions.
  • Match-specific information will be provided by BattleGrid.
